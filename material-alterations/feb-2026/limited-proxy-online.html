Submit Your Limited Proxy Online (SimplyVoting)

This page is a visual guide. The online portal uses labels like “Vote Now”, “Continue”, and “Confirm”. Follow the steps below to submit your selections.

Step-by-step

Online Submission Guide

If you are submitting online, you will typically receive an email from SimplyVoting with a link and your credentials.

Tip: On a phone, you may want to rotate to landscape when reading the screenshots.
1

Open the SimplyVoting email

Use the direct access link in the email (recommended). If needed, you can also use the site link + Elector ID / Password.

Direct access link (recommended)

Use the direct access link to open the portal immediately (no manual login).

2

From the Dashboard, select the Material Alterations item

Look for Vote Now – 2026 Material Alterations & Improvements.

Click “Vote Now”

Select the Material Alterations item to begin.

3

Answer Question 1 (Yes or No)

Select one option for Question 1, then proceed.

Select “Yes” or “No”

Choose one option for Question 1. Then click Continue (bottom-right).

4

Answer Question 2 (Yes or No), then Continue

Select one option for Question 2 and click Continue.

Select “Yes” or “No”

Choose one option for Question 2.

Click Continue

Proceed to the review screen.

5

Review your selections, then Confirm

If everything looks right, click Confirm.

Click Confirm

After you confirm, you may not be able to change your selections.

6

Success screen

Your submission is recorded. Click Back to Dashboard when done.

Back to Dashboard

You can exit back to the dashboard and (if available) view receipts.

Help

Need Assistance?

  • If you did not receive the SimplyVoting email, verify your email on file with the Association office and check your spam/junk folder.
  • If you start on a phone and the portal feels cramped, try a laptop/desktop browser.
  • If you have questions about the Limited Proxy questions themselves, return to the question pages for full project context and visuals.
