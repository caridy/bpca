Question 2 | Townhouse Poolside Facade

12-unit envelope/facade scope

What You Are Voting On

Question 2 asks owners to approve the townhouse poolside facade alteration for the 12 affected townhouse units, limited to envelope/facade scope.

This page consolidates all major owner-facing context in one place: scope limits, funding intent, visuals, and practical outcomes.

Project Details Visuals FAQ

Scope Boundary and Funding Intent

Included Scope

  • Poolside-facing envelope/facade update for 12 townhouse units.
  • Exterior-facing modernization alignment with broader property design direction.
  • Implementation within the townhouse side of the existing separation line.

Excluded Scope

  • No broad interior remodel scope is included in this vote question.
  • Existing fence line between pool deck and townhouse areas remains.
  • Existing planters/separation zone remain unchanged.

Cost Allocation Intent

The current proposal intent is that this townhouse facade scope is funded by the 12 affected townhouse units and does not create cross-subsidy to non-townhouse owners, subject to final legal package language.

Operational Context

This question is separated from the integrated pool-deck question so owners can independently decide this townhouse-specific facade scope while preserving clarity about who benefits and who pays.

If this question is not approved, base pool deck and other approved scopes can still proceed, and townhouse poolside facades remain as currently existing unless an alternative path is later approved.

FAQ | Question 2

Is this work in common area?

The affected townhouse side is not general common area in the same way as pool-deck open space. This question is structured as a townhouse-specific facade decision with townhouse-focused cost allocation intent.

Will this change the fence and planter separation between deck and townhouse units?

No. Existing fence and planters in the separation zone remain unchanged under this scope boundary.

Is this tied to the broader impact-window modernization effort?

Yes, the facade direction is intended to align with the property’s broader modernization and envelope upgrade direction.

Could this improve quality-of-life for adjacent townhouse residents?

Potentially yes. Upgraded poolside envelope elements may improve separation from nearby pool and court activity noise. Any acoustic benefit depends on final system specification and installation quality.
