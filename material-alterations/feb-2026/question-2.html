Included scope

Envelope/facade focus

  • Poolside-facing facade/envelope upgrade for the 12 townhouse units.
  • Updated floor-plan presentation is intentionally privacy-focused, keeping attention on the principal townhouse facade as viewed from the pool deck.
  • Alignment with modernization direction used in other visible areas of the property.
  • Integration with broader property envelope strategy, including impact-window direction.
  • Potential quality-of-life improvement for adjacent residents, including reduced exposure to pool/court noise depending on final system specification.
  • Creates a cleaner and more cohesive backdrop for the new modern pool deck, improving the overall arrival and amenity experience.
  • Helps screen ad hoc items currently visible from the deck side, reducing visual clutter along this highly exposed edge condition.

Excluded scope

What does not change

  • Pool deck open common-area program is not decided by this question.
  • Fence and planter separation between pool deck and townhouse side remain in place.
  • No general interior renovation package is included in this question.

Funding intent

Townhouse-specific cost allocation

Current proposal intent is that this scope is funded by the affected townhouse group, with allocation mechanics and implementation controls governed by final legal and Board-approved package language.

Operational context

Independent decision lane

This question is kept independent to preserve a clean owner decision on townhouse envelope scope without changing execution authority for other approved common-area work. At the same time, the proposed facade direction is designed to complement the pool deck renewal so the combined result reads as one coherent, modern composition rather than a mixed visual condition.

Frequently asked questions

FAQ

Is this the same as voting on the pool deck design?

No. This page covers only the townhouse poolside facade/envelope question.

Does this change the physical separation line at the pool deck edge?

No. Existing fence and planters separating townhouse side and deck side remain as-is under current scope boundary.

Who is expected to carry the cost?

Current proposal intent is townhouse-focused allocation for this scope, subject to final legal drafting and Board action.

Could this help with noise from pool and courts?

Potentially yes, depending on final glazing/system specs and installation quality. It is one of the expected practical benefits for adjacent townhouses.

How does this help the overall pool deck project outcome?

It improves visual consistency with the renewed pool deck design and helps eliminate the mixed, cluttered look currently visible along the townhouse edge, resulting in a cleaner and more cohesive shared environment.

If this question is not approved, what happens?

The townhouse poolside facade remains in existing condition unless an alternative proposal is approved in the future.

Should owners submit a vote even if they disagree?

Yes. Submitting a vote ensures your position is counted in the official tabulation.
